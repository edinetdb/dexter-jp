# Japan-market inputs

Read this reference only when selecting current Japan-specific DCF assumptions. It provides sourcing rules, not current market constants.

## Current market inputs

Obtain and date inputs from available authoritative sources:

- Risk-free rate: use a current Japanese government bond yield whose maturity matches the valuation horizon reasonably well.
- Equity risk premium: use a current, identified methodology and region/market basis; do not combine components from incompatible sources.
- Cost of debt: use company borrowing or bond evidence when available, with an explicit tax treatment.
- Tax rate: distinguish statutory, reported effective, and normalized forecast rates.
- Net debt and diluted shares: use the latest relevant reporting period and document inclusions, exclusions, treasury shares, and dilution.
- Current market price: use a verified price source and date. If unavailable, report it as unavailable.

## Company and market fit

Adjust assumptions for company-specific operating risk, leverage, currency exposure, and business mix using evidence. Peer or sector information may be a reasonableness check, but an undated sector range is not a substitute for a current WACC build.

Keep fiscal-year labels, valuation date, currency, and monetary units explicit. Do not present example values or historical ranges as current market facts.
